## What

`Resources/AppIcon.icon` is an Icon Composer package: five SVG layers on a deep indigo automatic gradient. The bulb and screw base are glass layers (the system renders refraction, specular highlights and translucency per appearance: light, dark, tinted, clear); a warm radial glow sits behind the bulb and an orange filament in front. `scripts/build-app.sh` compiles it with `actool` into `Assets.car` (what macOS 26+ renders) and a flat `AppIcon.icns` fallback for macOS 14–15.
